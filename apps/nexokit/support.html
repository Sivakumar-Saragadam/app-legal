Frequently asked questions

Helpful notes for using NexoKit.

What is NexoKit?

NexoKit brings everyday utilities together, including calculators, QR tools, currency conversion, weather, air quality, and unit conversion.

Why isn't a live result loading?

Weather, air quality, and currency features need an internet connection and may depend on external data services. Check your connection and try again.

How do I report a bug?

Email support with what happened, steps to reproduce it, your device model, and app version. Do not include passwords or sensitive information.

Can I suggest a feature?

Yes. Share the feature you have in mind and how you would use it. Feedback helps guide future improvements.

Do I need an account?

NexoKit's current experience is designed to be used without creating an account.
