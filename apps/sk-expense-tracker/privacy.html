Privacy Policy

SK Expense Tracker

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

SK Expense Tracker is committed to protecting your privacy. This application is designed to help you manage your personal finances while keeping your information secure and under your control.

2. Information We Collect

The application may store the following information that you enter:

  • Accounts
  • Income and Expense Transactions
  • Budgets
  • Categories
  • Application Preferences
  • Currency Settings

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal identification information.

3. Local Data Storage

All financial information entered into the application is stored locally on your device using a secure local database.

We do not upload your financial records to our servers.

4. AI Assistant

SK Expense Tracker includes an AI-powered financial assistant.

When you ask a question, only the financial information required to generate an answer may be securely transmitted to Google's Gemini AI service.

AI-generated responses are intended for informational purposes only and should not be considered financial, legal, tax, or investment advice.

5. Third-Party Services

The application uses the following third-party service:

  • Google Gemini API (Generative AI)

Use of the AI Assistant is subject to Google's privacy policies and terms of service.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not share your financial information with advertisers or marketing companies.

7. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect your information stored within the application. However, no electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Deletion

You may permanently delete all locally stored application data at any time by using:

Settings → Reset App Data

This action permanently removes your financial records from your device.

9. Children's Privacy

This application is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page.
